Remember the Sabbath day

The Seventh-Day Sabbath

A holy appointment with our Creator—resting in His finished work and delighting in His Word.

The Sabbath is a temple in time: a weekly invitation to cease from ordinary labour and turn our hearts toward worship, prayer, Scripture and communion with God.

Created and blessed by God

“And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it…” Genesis 2:3 KJV

The Sabbath began at creation. God rested on the seventh day, blessed it and set it apart. The fourth commandment calls His people to remember that holy time.

Jesus Christ is Lord of the Sabbath

“Therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.” Mark 2:28 KJV

The Sabbath points us to Christ, not away from Him. He taught that the Sabbath was made for man and showed mercy, healing and goodness upon it.

Rest is not the way of salvation

Keeping the Sabbath cannot purchase forgiveness or eternal life. We are saved by grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ. Obedience follows salvation; it does not earn it.

The Feasts of the LORD

God’s appointed times, proclaimed to be holy convocations.

“Concerning the feasts of the LORD, which ye shall proclaim to be holy convocations, even these are my feasts.” Leviticus 23:2 KJV

The weekly Sabbath and the annual appointed times belong to the LORD. Scripture specifies which days are holy convocations and days of rest.

Remembering deliverance through the blood of the lamb, fulfilled in Christ our passover.

The Gospel of Jesus Christ

The Bible Way to Heaven

Not by our works, but by God’s grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ.

1

All have sinned

Sin is the transgression of God’s law. Every person stands guilty before a holy God.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.” Romans 3:23 KJV
2

Sin brings death

We cannot erase our guilt through religion, good works, feast-keeping or Sabbath observance.

“For the wages of sin is death…” Romans 6:23 KJV
3

Christ died and rose again

Jesus bore our sins, was buried and rose bodily from the dead on the third day.

“Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures… and that he rose again the third day…” 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 KJV
4

Repent and believe

Turn to God, confess and forsake your sin, and trust wholly in the Lord Jesus Christ.

“Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved…” Acts 16:31 KJV
5

Call upon the Lord

Salvation is God’s gift. Come to Christ sincerely; no scripted words can substitute for true faith.

“For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.” Romans 10:13 KJV

A prayer of faith

Come honestly to the Lord Jesus

Lord Jesus Christ, I confess that I am a sinner and cannot save myself. I believe that You died for my sins, were buried, and rose again. I turn from my sin and place my faith in You alone. Forgive me, save me, and make me Yours. Help me to follow You according to Your Word. Amen.

Prayer does not save by its wording. The Lord Jesus Christ saves those who truly believe on Him.

Who we are

About Lord of the Sabbath Church

A KJV-centred Christian fellowship seeking to honour the Lord Jesus Christ through faith, truth and obedience.

Our purpose

We exist to proclaim the gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ, teach the Word of God, make disciples and encourage believers to walk faithfully with Him.

What guides us

We receive the Authorized King James Bible as our English authority for doctrine and practice. We remember the seventh-day Sabbath, study the appointed Feasts of the LORD and gather around Scripture, worship and prayer.

Christ at the centre

Our hope is not in religious observance. Forgiveness and eternal life are found in the Lord Jesus Christ alone. We desire that every teaching and practice lead people nearer to Him.
