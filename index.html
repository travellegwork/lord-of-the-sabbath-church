The Seventh-Day Sabbath

Created and blessed by God

“And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it…” Genesis 2:3 KJV

The Sabbath began at creation. God rested on the seventh day, blessed it and set it apart.

Jesus Christ is Lord of the Sabbath

“Therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.” Mark 2:28 KJV

Keeping the Sabbath cannot purchase salvation. We are saved by grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ; obedience follows salvation.
